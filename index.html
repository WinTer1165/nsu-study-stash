🤝 Want to contribute?

This stash grows when students share. You can add a course, fix faculty initials, or upload missing materials:

  1. Open an issue on the GitHub repo with the course code and what you have, or
  2. Fork & PR: add your entry to assets/courses.js following the existing format and open a pull request, or
  3. Email me the files (a Google Drive link works best) at aminulislamlabib@gmail.com and I'll add them with credit.

Please keep filenames clean and avoid uploading anyone's personal or graded work.

⚖️ Copyright & takedown

These materials are shared by students for educational use only. All lecture slides, notes and books remain the property of their respective authors, faculty and North South University.

If you are from NSU or a faculty member and would like any material removed, please email aminulislamlabib@gmail.com and it will be taken down promptly.
